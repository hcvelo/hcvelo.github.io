## To Santa Dash 

For those that want extra miles in their legs this is the ride to the Start of the CAFT Santa Dash.
It will be an easy ride up to Altrincham
(and the equivalent ride back after completing the 30 mile/48Km Santa Dash. A total ride of circa 108 Km)

### Date

Sunday, 29 Nov : 09:00

### Start

Middlewich Road, Holmes Chapel, Holmes Chapel, United Kingdom
